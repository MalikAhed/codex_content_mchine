## Owner's desired product
- A simple GitHub Pages review desk with watchable compact HTML stories, shared WebP scenes, compressed narration, readable captions, and downloadable decisions. No MP4 rendering or social uploads.
- One well-researched 5–8 minute landscape episode and three <60 second portrait shorts per case. Actual audio determines runtime; a hook/excerpt is never a complete episode. Missing narration is visibly labelled silent storyboard.
- Stylish tactile cinematic 3D reconstructions: pale faceless mannequins, charcoal/navy shadows, amber practical light, restrained red; varied plausible daylight too. Narrator black hoodie at wooden desk, mug/books/lamp. No HTML date/title cards on scenes. Dates belong in verified in-world props or captions/narration. No gore or invented factual dialogue.
- Every sentence has a source, caveat, relevant scene and exact measured audio boundaries. Route A uses whole image masters; Route B requires independently generated true-alpha subject and clean plate, never cutout masking. Narrator gentle +/-0.85 degree loop around desk anchor, 4.6 second period, subject to visual review. One audio playhead controls scenes/captions/motion and deterministic seeking.
- Primary evidence plus credible independent reporting; distinguish allegations, convictions, pardon and unresolved outcomes. Never label an unfetched source verified. No actionable crime instructions.
